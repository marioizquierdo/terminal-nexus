# Milestone 13 — The Nexus Pulse

**Status:** PLANNED
**Depends on:** Milestone 8 (the Commander, and the Nexus Pulse's dealing by schedule, step 8C); where it sits in the build order is Mario's call

The owner, 2026-10-07, once the in-match draft had its name: "Perhaps we will need to design a milestone dedicated
only to improving the Nexus Pulse experience, with dedicated UI and more specific powers." The Commander milestone
built how the Nexus Pulse deals — rarity and role, a schedule a level can change, chance, requirements and upgrades
([`commander-armies.md`](../game-design/commander-armies.md), how the Nexus Pulse deals) — on the popup the draft
already had. This milestone gives the Nexus Pulse its own moment on screen, and the powers to fill it.

## Question

Does each round open on a Nexus Pulse that feels like the Nexus speaking to its Commander — its own moment on
screen, a hand worth reading, a schedule a player plans around — without making a round slower to play?

## Steps

Not started. Each waits for Mario's word, and the order is a first guess.

### Step 13A — Its own moment on screen

- [ ] As a round opens, the Nexus Pulse shows itself before the Build Phase's plan: the hand, each card's rarity and
      role, in a layout made for it rather than a list in a popup.
- [ ] What the next hand will be dealt from is shown: the schedule, read a round ahead.
- [ ] A rarity reads at a glance, by a mark the monochrome floor keeps as well as by its style role.
- [ ] It works at 80 × 24, by keyboard, mouse and driver, and the pick stays optional until Mario says otherwise.

### Step 13B — More powers, and the legendary hand

- [ ] More of Vasse's designed powers are built, each with its rarity, role and modifiers: Countersigned as Standing
      Order's upgrade, Mutual Support Standard, Field Triage, Plating Revision, Emergency Procedure, Expedited
      Restoration, Early Warning — the effect kinds they need (`modifyRule`, `reveal`) with them.
- [ ] The hand every few rounds that deals legendaries: how often, and what a legendary may do.
- [ ] Every new power is proved in tests and played in a mission.

### Step 13C — A Commander bends the schedule

- [ ] An army may carry its own schedule, hand size or redraws (the short list in the design); one Commander uses
      one of them, played against the default.

## Out of scope

The run draft between battles (Milestone 11), new rosters (Milestone 12), and a required pick, which stays
Mario's call through the Nexus pick Experiment.

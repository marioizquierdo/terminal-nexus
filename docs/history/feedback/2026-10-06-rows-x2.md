>

>  t t t () () t

> ```
>
> that is not mandatory for moving, but units in the back would wait for units in the front to move one block away
> first, and if the units on top are perfectly aligned, they would also wait another turn so they form a grid,
> which is visually more sparse. The soace in the middle can still be taken, the sparse formation would be a
> preference, that increases the chances of reach distance be closer to equivalent between vertical and

**Scheduled** for the pull request after this one: units prefer a free column beside them and not to stand
straight above or below another, so a group spreads into a staggered grid. The preference never blocks a move,
and the space between can still be taken. That work explores what it costs the pathfinding, runs simulations, and
adds units and scenarios made to test formations facing up and down against formations facing sideways.

### F208 — Buildings have actions, not levels; units are never upgraded one at a time

> About building up grades, you are right. Let's formalize our current design as it is, not as we wish. Buildings
> don't have to have 1, 2, 3 level, because that will be forcing every single building to do that, and that may not be
> a good idea. Instead, buildings have some upgrades that can be applied through powers, and some buildings themselves
> have actions in the building. I think that's the thing that matters. And it would be great to make it visible to the
> player, but that is something that we will work through UX, another dedicated milestone or spike where we work
> through UX improvements. I will do that when I start playing. It will be natural to start constantly iterating that,
> so I'll probably have a session for UX. For now, we can say buildings can be selected and buildings themselves can
> have actions. So some actions that happen during build time, never during the game, where the building that was
> already created can be selected, and in that panel you can see a button to add a new feature to the building. Maybe
> for the barracks would be upgrade to upgrade production line, but it could have other things as well, and that could
> be per barracks. So it's not a general upgrade where all barracks get an upgrade. This will be the building itself
> gets an upgrade. Turrets are an obvious one. They should be easy to upgrade, just like any other turret game,
> basically. And then obviously there's some general upgrades that apply to all units or all buildings. Units do not
> have upgrades. You cannot select a unit and change things during build time. That just never happened. Like, things
> will happen either through powers or general construction or building upgrades on themselves as well, or actions.
> Buildings can also have a state change. Maybe there's some buildings that have two modes and you can select the
> building to change the mode. Maybe you want to have a banner that attracts units towards that, but then you can
> deactivate it or something. All of that can happen during build time and through the same interface by selecting the
> building and then seeing in the panel the different actions. That is the part that I want to formalize and that I
> want to make sure that our framework can absorb.

**Built.** Written down as the rule in [`commander-armies.md`](../../game-design/commander-armies.md) ("What
changes between rounds, and how"): buildings are selected and units never are; a building's actions are upgrades kept
by that building alone, modes switched between rounds, and orders for one round; general upgrades change every unit or
building of a kind; no building has levels. The framework holds all three: a building card declares its actions in its
army file, an upgraded or switched building is that building alone (its own content id, so the battle engine never
needed to change), Hold production is an order of the kind, and the Build Phase's card lists them with keys and
prices. First content: the Barracks's production line, the Turret's heavy rounds and its two firing modes.

### F211 — Double down on the RTS: the front stays, 30-second rounds, a regroup, and banners

> This is fantastic data. I am also leaning into double down the RTS style, leaving troops on the map, so they can
> keep moving forward. I think we can improve the troop movement to self-arrange in useful formations, have a better
> way to define aggressive vs defensive stance, clear movement and pathfinding algorithms, and predictable spawning
> schedules. With all that, we can safely leave the units on the map.
>
> Let's aim for this:
>
> * Shorter rounds: 30 seconds.
> * Keep units on the map after a fight. If they are in battle, they stop targeting, move back for 2 seconds, and
>   self-arrange in formation for 6 seconds (10 seconds total). This should happen during the build phase already.
> * It's ok to have troops moving during build phase, allow them to self arrange in formation, and move out when a
>   building is placed on top of them. But it's important that this is deterministic, so scripted battles stay
>   deterministic as well.
> * Aggressive vs Defensive stance: Yes I think this will be important. I want to formalize the idea of battle
>   banners. From simpler to complex:
>    * no banners, no stance (current): troops are always aggressive towards the enemy nexus. During campaign mode,
>      they have an automatic target (defined by the campaign) -- This is the simplest option but it doesn't leave
>      much room for strategic tactics.
>    * stance: a global setting to define aggressive, hold the line, or defensive stance -- Your initial suggestion,
>      this is simple enough, and already allows for a lot more dynamism.
>    * banner: a positional banner on the map, that can be placed as aggressive or defensive. By default, it is placed
>      on the enemy nexus as aggressive. If placed anywhere else on the map as aggressive the units move there first,
>      and then from there they keep going to the enemy nexus, which allows to define routes. Placed anywhere as
>      defensive, the units hold the line at that point, which allows for map control, or to defend your base.
>    * Commander and Secondary banners: two positional banners instead of one. The commander always follows the main
>      banner. Buildings can be selected to spawn units that follow the secondary banner instead. This allows for
>      complex tactics, which will be very important and interesting with resources and special bonuses that could be
>      held in the map. But it requires more UX details. I would like to eventually test this option, but we can
>      build incrementally towards this.

**Scheduled.** The question is answered (the front stays) and the decisions are in
[`core-loop.md`](../../milestones/core-loop.md). The runs milestone gains three steps before the route: the kept front
(30-second rounds, the regroup played during the Build Phase, units stepping out from under a new building), the
stance, and the banner. Two banners are written down as the direction and wait for the economy and held map bonuses.

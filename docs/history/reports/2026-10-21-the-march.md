# The march, survivors home and the mortar, measured

_Superseded by [commitment and reach, measured](2026-10-31-commitment-and-reach.md): the formation, commitment, reach, the flip-flop fix._

_2026-10-21. The whole spec ([`measurement.md`](../../system-design/measurement.md)) played again after the pathfinding
milestone's wrap-up: the march (the Commander as the army's guide, formation lines, the bomber a backliner),
survivors home on the roofs of the buildings that trained them, making way for a wave, the mortar's new numbers, and
faster routes. The result is [`2026-10-21-the-march.json`](../measurements/2026-10-21-the-march.json); compare it with
`node bin/measure.ts compare docs/history/measurements/2026-10-21-nexus-fights-back.json
docs/history/measurements/2026-10-21-the-march.json`. The comparison spans area damage too (the bomber, the mortar,
burning ground), built between the two and not played on the whole spec before._

The question, written before playing: **with the march, survivors home and the mortar's new numbers, do duels end as
they did, and does the side ahead after round 2 still win?**

## The first run found two things wrong

The first full run, on the round as the agents built it, was not kept. It found:

- **Round-1 knockouts were back**: 10 duels over in round 1 and 22 by round 2 (1 and 4 before), every one of them Dob's
  Nexus falling in a rush. The runner had been made a backliner, and an army of runners going beyond the line has no
  front: in the same 24 rush duels his Nexus fell in round 1 or 2 in 20 with the runner a backliner, and in 1 with the
  runner in the melee line. **The runner is melee again**; the bomber stays a backliner, and the march's scenarios show
  the line with a bench unit (the flanker).
- **The curve level's defended base jammed**: 16,935 unit-ticks blocked and 90 units stalled, where it had stood 1,501
  and none before survivors came home onto their roofs (measured on the commits before and after that change). Its
  target lies on its own base: survivors home on the roofs stood inside it, the next wave was held behind them, and
  making way sent every unit on every roof down into ground already full, even for a wave the army had no place for
  under its supply. **Now a roof keeps room for one wave** (a Barracks's six tiles less a wave's four troopers: two
  come home onto it, the rest round it), and **a unit makes way only for a wave its army has room for**. The level's
  fourth round, where it jammed worst, came back to 257 blocked unit-ticks against 210 before, its wave waiting 5
  ticks against 4.

**Duels end as they did.** The opening is still no knockout, the early lead still decides, and armies meet the enemy
about as spread as before; the march changes how an army arrives, not who wins.

| Pairing, over 36 duels each | Before | Now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Averno against Dob | 21 to 15 | 17 to 19 |
| Averno against Vasse | 24 to 12 | 28 to 8 |
| Vasse against Dob | 24 to 12 | 21 to 15 |

Dob gains in both his pairings, and Averno pulls further ahead of Vasse. Which change moved them is not separated here:
the mortar's numbers, the march and survivors coming home all touch these Commanders.

## The campaign

| Level and plan | Before | Now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PERIMETER, nothing built | won in round 3 | **lost in round 3** |
| PERIMETER, a defended base | won in round 3 | won in round 3 |
| The second level, either plan | won in round 3 | won in round 3 |
| The curve level, nothing built | lost in round 6 | lost in round 6 |
| The curve level, a defended base | won in round 6, 0 units stalled | won in round 6, 19 stalled |
| Averno's level, either plan | won in round 5 | won in round 5 |

**PERIMETER built-nothing was a near thing and now tips.** Played building nothing, its Nexus ended round 3 with 117
of 400 before this round, and with 44 under this round's other changes (the march among them: ranged units wait for
the melee, so the army reaches the line later); with survivors keeping room on the roof, one raider lives through round 2 and the Nexus falls in round 3.
A defended base still wins it with Vasse never falling.

**The curve level still stands blocked more than it did** (4,243 unit-ticks against 1,764 with a defended base), late
in the level, where a hundred and more units crowd a target on their own base. Smaller than the first run's 16,935;
not gone.

## The formation game

`node bin/measure.ts formations --seeds 1-6`, on the same build: four Barracks a side as a block, apart or a line.

| Against two mortars alone | Damage a block took | Apart | A line |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| With the mortar's first numbers ([area damage, measured](2026-10-21-area-damage.md)) | 120 | 77 | 87 |
| Now: a burst of 12 on the enemy alone, a fire of 8 a second for four seconds | 198 | 141 | 146 |

The mortar hurts more now, and a block still takes about one and a half times what an army built apart takes; the
mortars now kill a trooper in some games (none before). The bombers' numbers did not move (372 against 240). How tight
each shape meets the enemy is the same (3.1, 14.4 and 4.2 columns from its middle), and the shapes fought against each
other: a block beat apart 12 to 0, a line beat a block 10 to 2 and apart 10 to 2.

## What scripted players cannot say

Whether the march reads as an army arriving in order, whether a backliner is worth an army's slot, and whether a
first mission lost with nothing built is a bad thing or a fine one. Those are for playing.

## Question

Do base placement, automatic production, economy, drafting, persistence, and two asymmetric doctrines create a complete match worth replaying?

## Smallest intended artifact

Connect the proven presentation, Nexus Pulse, and Build Phase with deliberately tiny Citizen and Ravel Commander Armies. Each side begins with one Commander and the minimum meaningful worker production, military production, economy, supply, defense, outpost, and upgrade pool. Include a deterministic local opponent and several seeded maps.

This document must be expanded before implementation to select exact rosters, costs, timings, resources, maps, drafts, and rules. The full engine direction is not automatically its definition of done.

## Intended pass evidence

- at least two credible plans per army;
- Ravel pressure punishes greedy Citizens without deciding every match;
- Citizen fortification stabilizes without guaranteeing a late win;
- destroyed infrastructure and persistent casualties matter without making recovery hopeless;
- matches normally finish in 5–12 minutes;
- players can explain how Build Phase choices produced Pulse outcomes;
- at least one fresh player immediately wants another plan.

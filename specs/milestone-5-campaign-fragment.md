## Question

Can brief ASCII fiction, teaching, and unlocks turn proven matches into a compelling single-player campaign?

## Smallest intended artifact

A small Citizen origin fragment using proven Citizen/Ravel content, concise briefings, ASCII cutscenes, one or two meaningful unlocks, and one relationship change. It introduces the Activation and provisional human claim without attempting the complete campaign.

Before implementation, expand this document with exact missions, map fixtures, available Commander Armies, objectives, scenes, triggers, opponent policies, and unlocks.

## Intended pass evidence

The player understands the opening conflict, remembers the Citizen Commander and Ravel identity, knows why each unlock appeared, reaches play quickly on replay, and can summarize one answered question plus one larger mystery.

Only after this gate should production milestones be defined for complete rosters, broader campaigns, repeatable skirmish progression, Glitch/Feudals/Alder, sound, public mod packages, stronger opponent policies, multiplayer, or model-driven AI.
